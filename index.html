Brandi's Budget Helper

Brandi's Budget Helper is a private, personal tool for my own family budget, which I share with my spouse. It is not a product, is not available to anyone else, and is not sold or monetized.

What it does

It helps us plan each month and reconcile our accounts. It runs only on my personal computer. With read-only access, it reads our budget: account names, types and balances, categories and what's assigned to them, transactions with their payees and memos, scheduled transactions, and monthly totals. My AI assistant, Claude (made by Anthropic), reads that data in my private chats when I ask a question, for example which categories are overspent or which uncleared transactions explain a difference from the bank. It cannot change anything in the budget, and it never handles bank logins or bank credentials.

How the data is handled

The tool signs in through YNAB's official login (OAuth) with read-only permission, and its credentials are kept encrypted in my computer's keychain. Budget data is saved only on my computer, in a private folder that isn't synced to any cloud service. Card and account numbers are removed before anything is saved, and the location data YNAB keeps for payees is never requested. Data older than January 1 of the previous year is deleted automatically, and I delete all of it when I stop using the tool.

The data is used only for the planning and reconciling described above. It is never sold and never passed to anyone else. The only service that sees it is Claude, in my private chats, where Anthropic's privacy policy applies. YNAB collects and uses data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To have any data this tool holds deleted, contact me through github.com/brandimcn. It will be deleted from my computer.
